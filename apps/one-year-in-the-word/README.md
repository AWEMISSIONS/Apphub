# One Year in the Word

Buildless 365-day devotional and whole-Bible chapter schedule. Includes ESV reading links, contextual reflection prompts, local notes, completion tracking, year-plan navigation, and a notes index. The original Sites source could not be prepared; the live app's Day 1–4 outline was used to restore its visible structure, and the remaining daily schedule is regenerated from the Bible's chapter counts.

Published at `https://awemissions.github.io/Awe-faith/one-year-in-the-word/`.
